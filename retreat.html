Come to stillness.

We think retreat is an escape — a way to guard ourselves against the busyness that threatens us. Actually, retreat is the opposite. It is the stillness of an environment that reminds us we can trust ourselves, and that we do not have to be threatened by whatever happens in our lives. Retreat is simply the context of non-occurrence in which we can learn to discover appreciation for the distractions of everyday life.

Silent Meditation Retreats in the Shambhala Lineage

A container built for sustained practice. Over seven hundred acres of forest, field, and stream — with retreat cabins, meditation halls, and the silence to support practice from a weekend to three months. For practitioners ready to give themselves to the path.

This Is How

Weekthün

A seven-day silent group retreat — the first week of Dathün. Sitting and walking meditation from dawn to evening, oryoki-style meals, individual instruction, and talks by senior teachers. A complete container for formal practice.

Dathün

A twenty-eight-day silent month-long retreat. An in-depth immersion in mindfulness and awareness practice, making friends with who we are and drawing out our inherent fearlessness. The traditional long container.

Solitary Cabin Retreats

Individual retreat in one of seven secluded cabins deep in the woods — Yeshe, Vaishravana, Dhritarashtra, and others. For experienced practitioners with at least one year of daily practice and two weeks of group retreat behind them.
