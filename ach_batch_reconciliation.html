What "echeck" means in this database

The Omnis system does not use the term "echeck" as a category code. The closest match is AWD = "Automatic withdrawal", which is how recurring ACH/echeck debits are recorded. The payment categories in the system that touch electronic banking are:

CodeDescriptionNotes
AWDAutomatic withdrawalACH/echeck. Used for the 2026-04-30 batch.
CHECKCheckPaper check, not ACH.
CCCredit CardAuthorize.net / card payment.

Wherever "echeck" appears externally (bank statement, accounting system, etc.), the corresponding records in Omnis will be AWD entries.
