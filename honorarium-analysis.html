How "per year" is calculated from a non-annual sample

The data covers a 483-day window, which is 1.32 years. To project to a clean 12-month year:

Sample first program start:2025-01-03
Sample last program end:2026-04-30
Window length:2026-04-30 − 2025-01-03 + 1 day = 483 days
Annualization factor:365.25 days / 483 days = 0.7562
Modeled total (e.g., Base+20):$67,118.16 sum across 39 programs
Base+20 annualized:$67,118.16 × 0.7562 = $50,755.50/yr
Actual paid (full Xero):$60,114.17 from 59 transactions across 39 programs
Actual annualized:$60,114.17 × 0.7562 = $45,459.01/yr

Equivalent in everyday terms: the data shows 39 programs in 483 days, which is 2.4 programs/month. At that rate, a 12-month year would have ~29 programs, and the modeled or actual costs scale accordingly.

What this assumes, and how to validate it

Above: monthly program count across the window. Average 2.4 programs/month (navy dashed). If KCL's actual annual program flow is materially different from 12 × 2.4 = 29 programs/year, the annualized numbers should be re-scaled accordingly. Specifically:

  • If 2026 ramps to 3+ programs/month (Q1-Q2 2025 pace), expect ~15-20% higher annualized cost than shown.
  • If 2026 stays at 1-2 programs/month (Jan-Mar 2026 pace), expect ~30-40% lower annualized cost than shown.
  • The current 39-program average is roughly mid-range. Use these numbers as a planning anchor, not a precise forecast.

Methodology

Sample39 programs starting 2025-01-03 → ending by 2026-04-30. Filters: PROG_CATEGORY_CODE='REG', PROG_PRICE>$100, ≥3 arrived, not Test or Online.
Actual paidFull Xero GL 5250 total $60,114 (59 transactions, Jan 16 2025 – May 19 2026) divided across 39 sample programs ($1,541 average). Aggregate treatment avoids the per-program matching uncertainty.
Annualization× 365.25 / 483 = 0.7562. Applied uniformly to actual and modeled.
Net tuitionGross PROG charges − inline DISCOUNT − discount-flagged payments − DISC lines.
Multi-teacher rule6 of 39 programs have 2-3 teachers. Each teacher gets full per-night base; tuition % split evenly.
HeatmapsHypothetical scenarios using single-teacher math and gross tuition (no discounts). For comparing model shapes, not predicting specific program costs. Hover cells for the underlying formula.

Known limitations

Aggregate framingTreats $60k as total comp owed across all 39 programs. If some programs had no teacher payment owed, this overstates actual. If 22 no-Xero-match programs had teacher payments in non-program-ID Xero entries or other GL codes, actual is correctly captured.
Payment lagMedian 29.5 days post-program end. Programs ending late in window may have unrecorded Xero payments.
Annualization variance483-day sample × 2.4 programs/month average. Projection assumes future operations match this rate. ±15% uncertainty is realistic.
Operating costsNot modeled: food, kitchen, housekeeping, utilities, marketing, admin, teacher travel/lodging/meals.
