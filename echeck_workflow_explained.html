How echeck batches get into Omnis

Companion to the $1,746.39 reconciliation · prepared 2026-05-22

The short version: Karmê Chöling's recurring monthly donations are debited from donors' bank accounts via ACH (the system calls them AWD — Automatic Withdrawal). On the 22nd of each month, a staff member runs a script in Omnis that creates the payment records in advance based on each donor's recurring-donation profile. The bank then processes the actual ACH debits over the next 8 days, and any that fail get voided in Omnis on the same day they were created. The net amount the bank deposits to KCL's account on or about the 1st of the following month is the gross batch total minus failed echecks minus the bank's processing fee.

Your two questions, directly

1. How did this get into Omnis?

A staff member (logged in as spagan) ran an Omnis routine on 2026-04-22 that:

  • Read each active monthly-donor profile from the donation table (DONATION_TYPE = "MONTHLY")
  • Created a new payment record for each one, dated forward to 2026-04-30 (the intended settlement date)
  • Coded the payment as PAYMENT_CAT = "AWD" (Automatic withdrawal)
  • Tied each payment to the corresponding donation record via payment_detail.DONATION_ID
  • Submitted the batch file to the bank/ACH processor

The 9 failures came back during processor pre-validation. Whoever runs this batch went into Omnis the same day, marked those 9 rows as voided, and noted the processor's reason code in CC_AUTHO. The remaining 38 went through to settlement.

This is a recurring monthly process — you'll see a similar batch on the same date pattern every month, going back years.

2. Where do we get the bank statement to reconcile?

Omnis does not contain the bank-side record. The actual deposit confirmation and processing fee details live in the ACH processor's reporting interface, NOT in Omnis. Likely sources:

  • The ACH processor's portal. Common ACH/echeck processors used by small nonprofits include Authorize.net eCheck.Net, Stripe ACH, Bluepay, JackHenry/iPay, or a bank-direct ACH service. The processor's portal will have a batch report dated 2026-04-30 showing: gross batch total, per-item fees, batch fee, returns/NSFs, and the net deposit amount that was funded to KCL's bank account on or about 2026-05-01.
  • Karmê Chöling's bank statement. Look at the bank statement for the account that received the $1,746.39. The deposit line item will have a reference (often called an "ACH credit memo" or "merchant deposit") that includes the processor name and batch identifier. The bank statement won't show the breakdown, but it confirms the net number.
  • Email confirmation from the processor. Most ACH processors send a daily or per-batch settlement email. Search Karmê Chöling's email accounts (especially shared finance/bookkeeping inboxes) for "ACH", "echeck", "settlement", "batch", or the processor's name, around 2026-04-22 (batch submission) or 2026-04-30 to 2026-05-02 (settlement).

The fastest path: find who set up the AWD/echeck flow originally. The spagan user account that submitted the batch is your starting point — that person knows which processor KCL uses.

This data is "sort of backwards" because Omnis is the SOURCE-OF-INTENT, not the source-of-truth. Omnis says "here are the 47 echecks we asked the bank to process." The bank says "here is what we actually moved." Reconciling the two requires reading both. KCL has historically relied on the Omnis intent record, but the bank's actual deposit history is the authoritative financial record.

Suggested workflow for the new "Clearing Process - Echeck"

  • Each month after the AWD batch runs in Omnis (around the 22nd), pull the list of AWD payments with PAYMENT_DATE = the upcoming month-end.
  • When the processor settlement report arrives, save it alongside the Omnis batch list.
  • Reconcile: total settled in processor matches total non-voided in Omnis. Investigate any mismatch.
  • Book the gross donation revenue (e.g., $1,793.00 to GL 4810/4820 by donor designation).

  • Follow up with the failed donors so the $1,390 of intended monthly support is recovered or rescheduled.
