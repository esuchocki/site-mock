02 — Learn to Meditate

Begin gently.

Meditation has less to do with effort than we think. It is more about the simplicity of self-care, and trusting that we don’t have to force ourselves into any way of being. If you are effortless, that is love. And since love is unconditional, it has no boundary. Since anything without a condition is not a threat, space presents accommodation, which is great kindness. This is meditation.

Beginner Meditation Instruction in the Shambhala Lineage

An open door for the absolute beginner. The first steps of meditation, taught with patience and care in a fifty-year-old contemplative tradition. No experience needed. No prior reading. No vocabulary to learn before you arrive. Come as you are.

Ways to Begin

Open Meditation

Weekly drop-in meditation instruction. A teacher, a cushion, an hour of guided practice. No commitment, no cost, no experience required.

Weekend Introductions

A gentle two and three-day immersion. Live on the land, eat with the community, and meet the foundational practices of the Shambhala Buddhist path.

Foundational Courses

Multi-week courses for those ready to study more deeply. Structured progressions, ongoing teacher relationships, and the steady rhythm of returning to practice.
