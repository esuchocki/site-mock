A path begins here

Learn to meditate.

In the Shambhala tradition, meditation is not an escape from the world but a way of meeting it more fully. We offer instruction for those entirely new to the practice and for those returning after time away.

The instruction is simple. Sit upright. Notice the breath. When you find that your mind has wandered — and it will, again and again — gently return your attention to the breath. That is the practice. There is nothing else to attain.

What is harder is showing up. Sitting down on the cushion when you would rather be doing anything else. Returning the next morning after a difficult sit. The programs below are not designed to accelerate your path. They are designed to give you the environment, the community, and the instruction to make showing up a little easier.

Programs marked with an entry indicator are appropriate for beginners. No prior experience is necessary. Bring a willingness to sit.
