More Ways to Give

1. Planned & Legacy Giving

Some practitioners choose to extend their generosity beyond their lifetime. By including Karmê Chöling in your estate plans, you help sustain the retreat center, its teachings, and future generations of practitioners.

Planned gifts can also offer meaningful financial and tax benefits. Options may include:

  • A bequest in your will or trust
  • Naming Karmê Chöling as a beneficiary of a retirement account or life insurance policy
  • Charitable Gift Annuities

We encourage you to consult your financial and legal advisors to determine what is best for you. If you choose to include Karmê Chöling in your plans, please let us know at development@karmecholing.org so we can express our gratitude and ensure your intentions are honored.

If you are just beginning to explore planned giving, we are happy to connect you with John Murphy (jmartinmurphy@gmail.com), a Sangha member from Philadelphia and the Planned Giving Consultant for Shambhala Global. He is knowledgeable about a range of options, including Charitable Gift Annuities, and can help you understand how different vehicles may support both your financial wellbeing and your wish to give.

2. Gifts of Stock, Bonds, Securities, Mutual Funds

Donating appreciated stock is an easy and tax-wise way to support Karmê Chöling.

Step 1: Notify Us. Before you transfer shares, email us at development@karmecholing.org so we can properly track and acknowledge your gift. Please include your full name, the name of the stock, the number of shares, and your broker's name.

Step 2: We'll Provide Our Account Information for Your Broker. Your broker can transfer the shares directly to our Charles Schwab account.

Once the transfer is complete, we will send you a written acknowledgment for your tax records.

3. Employer Matching Gifts

Matching gift programs are a simple way to maximize your contribution at no additional cost to you. Many employers will match your charitable donation, potentially doubling your gift's impact.

How to get started: Check with your employer's human resources department to see if your company participates in a matching gift program. Many corporations, foundations, and even some small businesses offer this benefit to employees.

Need assistance? Email us at development@karmecholing.org so we can help you navigate the matching gift process and connect with your employer's program.

4. In-Kind Donations & Volunteer Service

Generosity takes many forms. In addition to financial contributions, Karmê Chöling gratefully welcomes in-kind donations and the gift of volunteer expertise.

"In-kind" support may include:

  • Skilled trades (carpentry, electrical, plumbing, landscaping)
  • Professional services (design, photography, marketing, technology)
  • Donated materials or equipment
  • Creative, administrative, and program support
  • Hands-on "sweat equity" for seasonal projects and campus improvements

Because we are a retreat center with ongoing facilities and program needs, skilled volunteer service can make a meaningful and lasting impact. Your time and expertise directly support the life of the center.

To learn about current needs or to schedule a volunteer project visit, please contact us at development@karmecholing.org.

Why Your Donation Matters

Your support helps keep the doors of Karmê Chöling open as a welcoming place for practice and community. With generous donations we are able to:

  • Offer deep retreat experiences across tradition and experience levels.
  • Maintain and care for our historic Vermont land and facilities.
  • Create access programs to widen participation and support those in need.

Every gift helps ensure that this sacred space continues to thrive as a refuge and place of learning.
