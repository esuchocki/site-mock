What this means for a whole year

Using the real booking mix (≈ paid stays/year, 2022–24 from the live database; staff/online/commuter excluded). Two honest numbers:

Guest-facing revenue (all-in)
True room-rate raise (food removed both sides)
Why two yearly numbers? The guest-facing number counts the food that's now itemized; the room-only number strips food off both the old and new side so you see the real room increase, not the accounting change. Food revenue itself didn't appear from nowhere — it was always collected, just hidden inside the old room price.

Room prices: room_inventory.csv (new) / room_inventory_old.csv (old). Booking mix: live legacy DB, 2022–24 average. The only assumptions are the two sliders above; everything else is the real rate tables and real booking counts.
